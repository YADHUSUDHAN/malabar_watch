# Malabar Watch - Safety & Portfolio Project Disclaimer

⚠️ **IMPORTANT NOTICE / സുപ്രധാന അറിയിപ്പ്:**

**Malabar Watch** is an open-source educational demonstration and portfolio software project designed to explore lightweight cloud infrastructure, zero-inbound security, open weather data processing, and resilient LLM reasoning.

### Key Points:
1. **NOT AN OFFICIAL EMERGENCY ALERT SYSTEM:**  
   Malabar Watch is **NOT** an official early warning tool, government disaster authority, or official forecasting service. It must **NEVER** be used as a primary or sole reference for life-safety decisions during flood or landslide emergencies.

2. **AUTHORITATIVE SOURCES:**  
   For all official weather forecasts, severe rainfall alerts, landslide warnings, and evacuation directives in Kerala, always refer strictly to official authorities:
   - **India Meteorological Department (IMD):** [https://mausam.imd.gov.in](https://mausam.imd.gov.in)
   - **Kerala State Disaster Management Authority (KSDMA):** [https://ksdma.kerala.gov.in](https://ksdma.kerala.gov.in)
   - Local District Administration & Collectorate Orders.

3. **EXPERIMENTAL RISK THRESHOLDS:**  
   The rainfall threshold values (24h/48h cumulative mm) used in this project are simplified illustrative heuristics based on public post-disaster literature. They do not constitute scientifically validated geotechnical warning thresholds.

4. **NO LIABILITY:**  
   The authors and contributors of Malabar Watch assume no responsibility or liability for any errors, omissions, delayed alerts, or actions taken based on information provided by this bot.
